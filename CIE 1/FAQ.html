Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for an event?

You can register by filling out the event registration form given above.

Is event registration free?

Yes, registration for most college events is free for CHARUSAT students.

Where will the event be conducted?

The venue is different for each event. Check the event card for the venue details.

Can I participate in more than one event?

Yes, students can participate in multiple events if their schedules do not overlap.
